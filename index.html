Nat-Geo

One of my favorite shots happened on a freezing morning in Gulmarg. I had no plan—I just came and cold fingers—when I stumbled upon this lone tree standing tall in a snowy dreamscape.

Just as I framed the shot, a flock of birds flew by, perfectly timed. That photo ended up being featured by National Geographic.

It still gives me goosebumps—not just from the cold, but from the reminder that sometimes the best moments come when you least expect them.

Magic happens when you follow your gut.

Gulshan Bookstore

When Gulshan Books reached out, I knew this wasn't just another video project. Filming Kashmir's oldest bookstore felt like capturing a living piece of history.

From sunlit reading corners to shelves packed with stories, every frame held magic.

The team trusted me to tell their story—and that trust made it special.

Projects like this remind me why I fell in love with videography in the first place.

Café 2 Bytes

This project began over coffee and conversation at Café 2 Bytes, where I set out to understand more than just visuals—I wanted to capture the café's spirit.

It wasn't just a shot; it was about observing the real, everyday moments that made the place special.

From quiet first dates to familiar regulars, I focused on the warmth and honesty of the space.

The emphasis shifted from technical angles to atmosphere and emotion. The final images reflected the café's soul.

Wedding Stories
